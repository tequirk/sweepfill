# User Guide

## Overview

Follow the sweepstakes you like, and Sweepfill keeps a daily checklist of the ones you can enter today. Open each one, enter it, and Sweepfill keeps track.

## Opening Sweepfill

Click the Sweepfill button in your browser's toolbar to open it in a panel beside the page. It stays open beside the page while you enter contests.

## Creating an Account

Sign up with your email address and a password. Your account keeps the contests you follow and your entries in sync.

## Filling In Your Profile

Add your name, address, phone, birthdate and any custom fields under Profile. Sweepfill uses your state and age to hide contests you aren't eligible for. Your profile stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## Finding Contests

Discover lists the contests you're eligible for. Follow the ones you want to enter, and skip the ones you don't so they stay out of your way.

A free account can follow up to five running contests at a time.

## Entering Today's Contests

Today shows the contests you follow that are due, based on how often each contest's official rules let you enter. Open a contest, fill in the form and press submit yourself, then tick it off. The toolbar badge counts the contests still due.

## Auto Check-Off

Turn on Auto check-off in the menu and Sweepfill will tick a contest off for you when it sees your entry went through, such as a "Thanks for entering" page. Your browser will ask you to allow Sweepfill on all sites, since a contest can confirm your entry on a different website from the one you entered on. Sweepfill only pays attention to tabs you open from Today.

If it isn't sure, Sweepfill will ask whether you entered.

## Past Contests

Past Contests keeps a summary of contests that have ended: the prizes, how many times you entered, and whether you won. Mark a contest as won to remember it.
