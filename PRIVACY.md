# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Sweepfill browser extension (hereby referred to as "Application"), and the account and services that come with it, created by Tequirk LLC (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

In short: your personal details (name, address, phone, birthdate) stay in your browser and are never sent to the Service Provider. If you use your browser's own sync, your browser copies them to your other signed-in browsers. Their server keeps your account, the contests you follow, and when you entered them. The Service Provider does not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics trackers.

## Information Kept on Your Device

These are kept in your browser's extension storage and are never sent to the Service Provider:

* **Your profile:** name, email, phone, birthdate, address, and any custom fields you add. The Application uses your state and age to hide contests you aren't eligible for. Your profile is kept in your browser's sync storage: if you have turned on sync in your browser (for example, by signing in to Chrome or Firefox), your browser copies it to your other browsers signed in to the same browser account, so it's there on a new computer or after reinstalling. That copy is held by your browser's maker (Google or Mozilla) under their own privacy policy, never by the Service Provider. With browser sync off, it stays on this device.
* **A second person's profile, if you add one:** with a paid plan you can add the profile of one other person in your household, to enter contests for them too. It holds the same details as yours and is kept the same way, in your browser's sync storage, never on the Service Provider's server. Make sure they're happy for you to keep their details and enter for them.
* **Today's list**, saved so it opens when you're offline, plus check-offs made offline until they can be sent.
* **The tabs you open from Today**, and what the Application noticed in them, while they're open.
* **Contests you skip** in Discover, so they stay out of your way.
* **Your settings**, such as the theme and whether Auto check-off is on.

Removing the Application from your browser deletes all of this from that browser. A copy of your profile kept by your browser's sync may remain in your browser account.

## Information Collection and Use

When you create an account, these are stored in the Service Provider's database, hosted by Supabase:

* **Your account:** your email address and password. Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form.
* **The contests you follow**, and when you added them.
* **Your entries:** which contest, when, and whether you ticked it yourself or the Application checked it off. If your account has a second person's profile, each entry also carries a random code saying whose it was, never their name or other details, so each of you sees what you still have to enter. Removing that person from your account deletes their entries.
* **Your contest history:** when a contest is deleted (30 days after it ends), a one-line summary of it is kept for you: the contest's title, sponsor and prizes, how many times you entered, and whether you marked it as won.
* **Your daily progress:** for each day, how many contests were on your list, how many you entered, and what the entered contests' prizes are worth. It's what your streak and your weekly numbers are worked out from. Counts only: which contests, and when you entered them, aren't kept here. It's shared by everyone on your account.
* **Your plan:** whether you have a paid subscription, its billing length, its renewal date (or the date it ends, if you cancel), any promotion code used, and the Stripe customer and subscription numbers that link it to your payments. Payments are handled by Stripe, which receives your account's email address to set up the subscription; the Service Provider never sees or stores your card details.
* **Your referral code, if you enter one:** the code, which Sweepfill admin it belongs to, and when you entered it. A referral code is a discount code you can enter before paying for Sweepfill. Admins are people who work with the Service Provider to find contests and bring people to Sweepfill. Entering a code is optional and can't be changed afterwards. If you pay with it, you count as that admin's member: they see only totals about their paying members, such as how many there are and how many entries they made. They never see who you are, your email address, or anything about you alone.

This information is used only to run the Application, and to send you emails about your account, such as the code you're sent to create an account or reset your password.

## Auto Check-Off

Auto check-off is off unless you turn it on. When it's on:

* **Your browser asks you to allow the Application on all sites.** A contest can confirm your entry on a different website from the one you entered on, so the Application can't know in advance where it needs to look.
* **It only pays attention to tabs you open from the Application's Today list**, and tabs those open. Your other browsing is ignored.
* **In those tabs, the Application reads the page in your browser** to spot signs that your entry went through, such as "Thanks for entering". It also notices when you press a button, and when the page sends a form. The browser reports form submissions from every tab; the Application ignores those not opened from Today. It looks inside a submission only for the contest's ID, so it knows which contests were entered. Nothing you typed is kept.
* **To improve automatic check-off, the Application sends the Service Provider a short report** about each entry from a tab opened from Today. The report contains which contest, how the entry was recorded (automatically, confirmed by you, ticked by you, undone, or not entered), and a few yes/no answers, such as "did the page change after you pressed the button?". Unless you turn on **Help improve Auto check-off** (below), the database refuses reports containing page text, web addresses, button labels, or anything you typed. Reports are deleted when the contest is deleted.
* **Help improve Auto check-off is off unless you turn it on**, in Settings. When the Application misses an entry of yours, the report also carries what your contest tab showed afterwards, so the Service Provider can teach the Application to recognise that contest's confirmation for everyone: short lines of text from the page (such as "Thanks for entering!"), the names of buttons you pressed, and the site of the page you landed on (such as sweeps.example.com, never the page's address). Before it leaves your browser, anything holding a detail from your profile or saved answers, an email address, a long number or a phone number is removed. The Service Provider's staff only ever see a line, button or site once at least 3 different people have reported it for the same contest, and these details are deleted after 30 days. Nothing you typed into a form is sent.

Turning Auto check-off off gives the all-sites access back to your browser, and stops the reports.

## What the Application Does Not Do

* It does not sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.
* It does not use your data for anything other than running the Application.
* It does not use analytics or tracking services.
* It does not run code downloaded from the internet. Everything the Application runs is in the extension your browser installed.
* It never submits a contest form for you. You always press submit yourself.

## Opt-Out Rights

You can turn off Auto check-off at any time, which stops the reports and gives the all-sites access back to your browser. You can turn off Help improve Auto check-off at any time too, which stops sending what your contest tabs showed.

You can remove everything the Application keeps in your browser by removing the Application from your browser. To remove what's stored on the server as well, delete your account as described below.

## Data Retention Policy

* **Contests, your follows, entries and reports** are deleted automatically 30 days after a contest ends.
* **What your contest tabs showed**, if you turned on Help improve Auto check-off, is deleted from reports after 30 days.
* **Your account, contest history, daily progress and referral code** are kept until you delete your account.
* **To delete your account** and everything stored with it, open Settings from the Application's menu and use **Delete account** at the bottom. This deletes your account, the contests you follow, your entries, contest history, daily progress and reports from the Service Provider's server at once, and clears your profile, and any second person's, from your browser and its synced copies. Any paid subscription is cancelled at the same time, with no further charges. If you can't sign in, email support@sweepfill.com from the address on your account, and the Service Provider will respond in a reasonable time.
